1. Purpose

This registry defines the controlled vocabularies used across Morning Star.

No document may silently introduce an equivalent replacement for a canonical state, role, authority class, verification result, reconstruction state, or constitutional disposition.

Every canonical vocabulary revision must identify:

affected term;
prior term;
proposed term;
rationale;
semantic impact;
dependency impact;
affected artifacts;
approval authority;
effective version;
supersession record.
15. Constitutional Result

Morning Star retains one controlled semantic surface across its architecture, procedures, registers, validation rules, and future implementations.
