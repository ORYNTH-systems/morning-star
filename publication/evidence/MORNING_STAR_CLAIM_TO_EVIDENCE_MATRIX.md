# 1. Governing Purpose

This document determines, claim by claim:

- what is constitutionally established;
- what is formally derived;
- what is implemented;
- what has been validated;
- what requires external literature;
- what remains prospective;
- what scholarly language the available evidence permits;
- what overstatement the available evidence prohibits.

This matrix does not redefine Morning Star.

It evaluates the evidentiary status of claims derived from the frozen Morning Star constitutional core.

every constitutional claim has a stable Claim ID;
every implementation claim has been mapped to an exact artifact or marked unsupported;
every validation claim identifies a completed record or remains expressly unvalidated;

every prospective claim is clearly distinguished from established results;
every repository reference includes an exact path and governing version;
permitted language is bounded by the available evidence;
prohibited overstatement is specified for every material claim;
contradictions are escalated rather than silently resolved;
the matrix has passed constitutional and evidentiary read-back.
