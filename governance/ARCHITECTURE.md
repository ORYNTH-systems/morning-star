## 1. Constitutional Subject

Morning Star governs preservation of canonical meaning from first encounter through competent participation in the ORYNTH ecosystem.

Its constitutional concern is not merely education, documentation, onboarding, or access.

Its concern is the preservation of:

- canonical identity;
- framework identity;
- dependency order;
- authority;
- provenance;
- uncertainty;
- interpretation class;
- participation boundaries;
- revision continuity;
- auditability.

## 9. Constitutional Invariants

The Morning Star architecture must preserve the following invariants:

### MS-INV-001 — Canonical Identity Preservation

A governed object must remain identifiable through every transformation and interpretation.

### MS-INV-002 — Framework Identity Preservation

Distinct ORYNTH frameworks must not be silently merged.

### MS-INV-003 — Dependency Order Preservation

A dependent state must not precede satisfaction of its mandatory prerequisites.

### MS-INV-004 — Authority Separation

Access, familiarity, competency, authorship, and authority must remain distinct.

### MS-INV-005 — Provenance Preservation

Every governed transformation must remain reconstructable.

### MS-INV-006 — Uncertainty Preservation

Unknown, unresolved, conflicting, and uncertain states must not be silently converted into certainty.

### MS-INV-007 — Interpretation Classification

Derived meaning must not be represented as canonical meaning without governed admission.

### MS-INV-008 — Participation Evidence

Participation roles must not be granted without sufficient evidence.

### MS-INV-009 — Role-Bounded Action

An actor must not exercise authority beyond the actor's admitted role and scope.

### MS-INV-010 — Revision Continuity

Changes to canonical objects must trigger declared downstream review and revalidation.

### MS-INV-011 — Drift Reconstruction

Semantic drift must be preserved, identified, and reconstructed rather than silently overwritten.

### MS-INV-012 — Deterministic Constitutional Decision

Equivalent constitutional inputs must produce equivalent constitutional decisions.

### MS-INV-013 — Trace Completeness

No terminal constitutional decision may exist without a reconstructable trace.

### MS-INV-014 — Failure Distinction

Failure, deferral, refusal, uncertainty, suspension, revocation, and incompletion must remain distinct.

### MS-INV-015 — Constitutional Supremacy

No runtime, interface, implementation, or derived artifact may override the Constitution.

## 18. Architecture Freeze

Architecture freeze means:

- the five-volume constitutional structure is fixed;
- the governing dependency sequence is fixed;
- the constitutional subject is fixed;
- the invariant family is fixed;
- changes require governed amendment;
- implementation may begin without redefining the constitutional foundation.

Freeze does not prohibit correction.

Correction must follow amendment and trace requirements.
