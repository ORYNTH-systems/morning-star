# Morning Star Observer State Machine

**Document Status:** Constitutional Candidate  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Governance Domain:** Semantic Entry and Competent Participation

---

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the canonical observer-state architecture governing progression from first exposure to competent stewardship within the ORYNTH ecosystem.

The state machine prevents exposure, familiarity, interpretation, agreement, or repetition from being falsely treated as understanding.

---

## 2. Constitutional Principle

An observer may not acquire a participation state merely by encountering information.

State advancement requires demonstrated preservation of:

- canonical meaning;
- framework identity;
- dependency order;
- constitutional boundaries;
- explicit uncertainty;
- source traceability;
- distinction between interpretation and canonical definition.

---

## 3. Canonical Observer States

### OS-00 — UNEXPOSED

The observer has not encountered the governed object.

No interpretive or participatory claim is admissible.

### OS-01 — EXPOSED

The observer has encountered one or more representations of the governed object.

Exposure establishes contact only.

Exposure does not establish comprehension.

### OS-02 — ORIENTING

The observer is identifying:

- the governed object;
- its constitutional domain;
- its location within the ORYNTH ecosystem;
- its relationship to adjacent frameworks;
- the distinction between source material and interpretation.

### OS-03 — OBSERVING

The observer is examining the governed object without yet claiming competent reconstruction.

The observer may identify questions, ambiguities, dependencies, and apparent relationships.

### OS-04 — DIFFERENTIATING

The observer demonstrates the ability to distinguish:

- theory from protocol;
- protocol from runtime;
- runtime from implementation;
- implementation from evidence;
- evidence from interpretation;
- narrative representation from constitutional definition;
- one ORYNTH framework from another.

### OS-05 — RECONSTRUCTING

The observer can reconstruct the governed meaning from authoritative sources while preserving:

- canonical terminology;
- dependency sequence;
- scope;
- exclusions;
- uncertainty;
- provenance.

### OS-06 — VERIFIED_COMPREHENSION

The observer has demonstrated structurally adequate understanding.

Verified comprehension requires more than verbal agreement or surface summary.

### OS-07 — PARTICIPATING

The observer may contribute within the boundaries of the verified domain.

Participation remains role-bounded and scope-bounded.

### OS-08 — STEWARDING

The observer may preserve, transmit, review, or govern the architecture for subsequent observers.

Stewardship does not authorize unilateral redefinition.

### OS-09 — SUSPENDED

Participation or stewardship authority is temporarily inactive because required semantic integrity, evidence, eligibility, or role conditions are unresolved.

### OS-10 — REVOKED

Previously admitted participation or stewardship authority has been withdrawn.

Revocation does not erase historical provenance.

```text

    ↓

    ↓

    ↓

The canonical sequence represents the maximum admissible progression.

A state may be paused, suspended, denied, or reversed where semantic integrity cannot be demonstrated.

5. Transition Invariant

For any observer o, governed object g, and proposed transition from state s_i to s_j:

AdmissibleTransition(o, g, s_i → s_j)
=
Eligibility
∧ RequiredEvidence
∧ DependencyIntegrity
∧ IdentityPreservation
∧ BoundaryPreservation
∧ Traceability
∧ UncertaintyPreservation

No transition is admissible when any required term evaluates false or unresolved.

6. Prohibited State Substitutions

The following substitutions are constitutionally invalid:

Exposure ≠ Orientation
Orientation ≠ Differentiation
Differentiation ≠ Reconstruction
Reconstruction ≠ Verified Comprehension
Agreement ≠ Verified Comprehension
Fluency ≠ Verified Comprehension
Participation ≠ Stewardship
Authorship ≠ Constitutional Authority
Confidence ≠ Competence
7. Regression

Observer state may regress when:

canonical terms are repeatedly conflated;
framework identity is lost;
dependency order is reversed or ignored;
interpretation is presented as canonical authority;
uncertainty is erased;
provenance cannot be reconstructed;
unauthorized revisions are propagated;
role boundaries are exceeded.

Regression must be recorded as a governed state transition.

8. State Evidence

Every admitted state above EXPOSED must have evidence sufficient to reconstruct:

who assessed the state;
which governed object was assessed;
which criteria were applied;
what evidence was evaluated;
what uncertainty remained;
when the state was admitted;
whether the state remains active.
9. State-Machine Boundary

This state machine governs semantic participation eligibility.

It does not govern:

personal worth;
spiritual status;
intelligence;
moral value;
social rank;
private belief;
identity outside the governed domain.
10. Constitutional Result

The canonical result of this state machine is not conformity.

The canonical result is competent participation without semantic corruption.
