Morning Star Stewardship Governance

Document Status: Constitutional Candidate
Version: 0.1.0
Governance Identifier: MS-SG-001

1. Purpose

This document governs the transition from competent participation to stewardship.

Stewardship is the bounded authority to preserve, transmit, review, and govern a defined portion of the ORYNTH architecture.

2. Stewardship Principle

Stewardship is preservation authority, not ownership of meaning.

A steward may not silently replace the governed architecture with personal preference, institutional convenience, derivative interpretation, or reputational authority.

3. Stewardship Eligibility

A steward candidate must demonstrate:

verified comprehension;
sustained framework differentiation;
reliable dependency preservation;
traceable use of authoritative sources;
explicit uncertainty handling;
semantic-drift detection;
revision discipline;
role-boundary compliance;
ability to reconstruct the architecture for another observer.
4. Stewardship Classes
SG-01 — DOCUMENT STEWARD

Preserves a defined canonical document or artifact.

SG-02 — FRAMEWORK STEWARD

Preserves a defined ORYNTH framework and its constitutional relationships.

SG-03 — IMPLEMENTATION STEWARD

Preserves implementation fidelity without acquiring authority to redefine the governing theory or protocol.

SG-04 — VERIFICATION STEWARD

Conducts bounded verification under an authorized specification.

SG-05 — AUDIT STEWARD

Conducts traceable assessment without acquiring ownership over the assessed architecture.

SG-06 — ENTRY STEWARD

Guides observers through Morning Star without coercion or unauthorized simplification.

SG-07 — CONSTITUTIONAL STEWARD

Exercises explicitly delegated authority over constitutional maintenance.

This role requires direct authorization.

5. Stewardship Invariants

Every steward must preserve:

Canonical Meaning
∧ Framework Identity
∧ Dependency Order
∧ Constitutional Boundary
∧ Provenance
∧ Explicit Uncertainty
∧ Revision Traceability
6. Prohibited Steward Conduct

A steward may not:

present interpretation as canonical text;
remove attribution or provenance;
conceal material uncertainty;
collapse distinct ORYNTH frameworks;
authorize participation outside delegated scope;
revise constitutional objects without authority;
use stewardship status as personal rank;
prevent legitimate criticism;
require ideological conformity;
claim that disagreement alone constitutes semantic failure.
7. Revision Governance

A steward proposing a revision must record:

the governed object;
current canonical state;
proposed state;
rationale;
evidence;
affected dependencies;
compatibility impact;
unresolved uncertainty;
authorizing role;
review disposition;
effective version.

No revision becomes canonical merely because it has been written, published, implemented, or repeated.

8. Transmission Requirement

When transmitting governed material, the steward must distinguish:

canonical content;
explanatory material;
interpretation;
analogy;
extension;
criticism;
unresolved question;
superseded content.
9. Stewardship Suspension

Stewardship may be suspended when:

semantic integrity cannot be demonstrated;
scope boundaries are exceeded;
authority cannot be verified;
required evidence is unavailable;
revision provenance is incomplete;
material conflicts remain unresolved.

Suspension preserves the historical record.

10. Stewardship Revocation

Revocation requires a traceable disposition identifying:

revoked authority;
affected domain;
reason;
evidence;
effective time;
responsible authority;
downstream remediation requirements.
11. Succession

A stewardship succession is admissible only when the incoming steward can independently reconstruct:

the governed object;
its dependencies;
its revision history;
its unresolved questions;
its active risks;
its constitutional constraints.
12. Morning Star Terminal Boundary

Morning Star reaches its terminal responsibility when an observer has become an eligible steward capable of preserving entry integrity for subsequent observers.

Morning Star does not govern every experience, interpretation, narrative, simulation, or world encountered after initiation.

Those downstream domains must be governed by their own constitutional architectures.

13. Constitutional Result

Stewardship completes the Morning Star entry cycle:

Observer
→ Oriented Observer
→ Reconstructing Observer
→ Verified Participant
→ Bounded Steward
→ Preservation of Entry for the Next Observer
