Morning Star Canonical Entry Protocol

Document Status: Constitutional Candidate
Version: 0.1.0
Protocol Identifier: MS-CEP-001

1. Purpose

The Canonical Entry Protocol governs movement from first contact with the ORYNTH ecosystem to eligible, competent, and traceable participation.

The protocol prevents premature participation from becoming a source of semantic drift.

2. Protocol Sequence
Contact
→ Intake
→ Eligibility Assessment
→ Orientation
→ Dependency Mapping
→ Framework Differentiation
→ Canonical Reconstruction
→ Verification
→ Role Assignment
→ Participation
→ Stewardship Eligibility
3. CEP-01 — Contact Registration

Record:

observer identifier;
entry source;
entry timestamp;
first governed object encountered;
representation type;
known provenance;
initial uncertainty.

Contact registration does not imply consent, competence, or participation.

4. CEP-02 — Intake Classification

Classify the observer's intended relationship to the ecosystem:

general observer;
reader;
researcher;
reviewer;
implementer;
auditor;
collaborator;
steward candidate;
unspecified.

Classification remains provisional until verified.

5. CEP-03 — Entry Eligibility

Entry eligibility evaluates whether the available materials and observer conditions are sufficient for governed orientation.

EntryEligible
=
SourceAvailability
∧ MinimumProvenance
∧ DomainIdentifiability
∧ DependencyDiscoverability

Where any term is unresolved, the result must remain unresolved rather than defaulting to eligible.

6. CEP-04 — Orientation

Orientation must establish:

what ORYNTH is;
what the currently governed object is;
where that object sits within the ecosystem;
what constitutional authority governs it;
which claims are canonical;
which claims are provisional;
which dependencies must be encountered first.
7. CEP-05 — Dependency Mapping

The observer must identify required antecedent structures before downstream interpretation is admitted.

A downstream object may not be treated as self-originating when its meaning depends upon prior ORYNTH architecture.

8. CEP-06 — Framework Differentiation

The observer must distinguish, where applicable:

foundational theory;
constitutional architecture;
protocol;
runtime;
verification method;
audit method;
implementation;
application;
narrative substrate;
derivative interpretation.
9. CEP-07 — Canonical Reconstruction

The observer reconstructs the governed object using authoritative sources.

The reconstruction must preserve:

canonical subject;
purpose;
scope;
exclusions;
invariants;
dependencies;
terminology;
uncertainty;
evidence chain.
10. CEP-08 — Initiation Verification

Verification determines whether the reconstruction is structurally competent.

Verification must not be reduced to agreement, memorization, or stylistic imitation.

11. CEP-09 — Role Assignment

Role assignment must be:

explicit;
domain-bounded;
evidence-backed;
revocable;
traceable;
separated from personal identity.

No role grants authority beyond its declared domain.

12. CEP-10 — Participation Admission

Participation is admitted only when:

ParticipationAdmissible
=
VerifiedComprehension
∧ ActiveRole
∧ ScopeAlignment
∧ DependencyIntegrity
∧ Traceability
∧ NoBlockingViolation
13. CEP-11 — Stewardship Eligibility

Stewardship eligibility requires demonstrated ability to:

preserve canonical meaning across transmission;
identify semantic drift;
reconstruct evidence chains;
distinguish authority from interpretation;
preserve uncertainty;
govern revisions;
prevent unauthorized canonicalization.
14. Protocol Outputs

The protocol produces one of the following:

ENTRY_NOT_STARTED;
ENTRY_IN_PROGRESS;
ENTRY_BLOCKED;
ORIENTATION_COMPLETE;
RECONSTRUCTION_PENDING;
VERIFICATION_PENDING;
PARTICIPATION_ADMISSIBLE;
PARTICIPATION_NOT_ADMISSIBLE;
STEWARDSHIP_ELIGIBLE;
STEWARDSHIP_NOT_ELIGIBLE;
INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE;
INDETERMINATE.
15. Noncoercion Rule

Morning Star governs admissibility within the ORYNTH ecosystem.

It does not compel belief, agreement, participation, identity adoption, or continued engagement.

16. Constitutional Result

The Canonical Entry Protocol converts access into governed orientation and converts verified understanding into bounded participation.
