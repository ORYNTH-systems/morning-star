# Morning Star Constitutional Amendment Procedure

**Document ID:** MS-GOV-AMEND-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** CONSTITUTIONAL CONVERGENCE  

## 1. Purpose

This procedure governs every proposed modification to the Morning Star Constitution and its canonical constitutional registries.

## 2. Required Amendment Record

Every amendment must possess:

- Amendment ID
- Proposal date
- Proposing authority
- Constitutional provisions affected
- Registries affected
- Rationale
- Evidence
- Dependency impact
- Role impact
- State-machine impact
- Authority impact
- Backward-compatibility impact
- Required migration
- Verification plan
- Decision
- Effective version

## 3. Amendment States

The canonical amendment lifecycle is:

1. PROPOSED
2. INTAKE_VALIDATED
3. IMPACT_ANALYSIS
4. CONTRADICTION_REVIEW
5. VERIFICATION_PENDING
6. VERIFIED
7. APPROVED
8. RELEASED
9. SUPERSEDED
10. REJECTED
11. WITHDRAWN

## 4. Admission Conditions

An amendment may be approved only when:

- the proposing authority is identified;
- the constitutional need is stated;
- affected provisions are enumerated;
- prohibited conflations are reviewed;
- dependency effects are evaluated;
- authority ownership remains explicit;
- uncertainty is preserved;
- required migrations are specified;
- verification succeeds;
- the amendment does not silently erase constitutional history.

## 5. Amendment Refusal

An amendment must be refused or returned unresolved when it:

- expands authority without constitutional basis;
- destroys provenance;
- suppresses unresolved uncertainty;
- creates an ungoverned role or state;
- merges prohibited concepts;
- bypasses dependency order;
- invalidates historical reconstruction;
- lacks sufficient evidence;
- cannot identify affected downstream artifacts.

## 6. Release Requirements

An approved amendment requires:

- updated constitutional artifacts;
- updated registries;
- migration instructions;
- validation evidence;
- changelog entry;
- release manifest;
- version increment;
- Git commit;
- release tag where applicable.
