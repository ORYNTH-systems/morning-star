# Morning Star Initiation Governance Rules

**Document ID:** MS-V5-GOV-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0  
**Status:** INITIATION FOUNDATION  

## Evaluation Order

Every initiation decision must be evaluated in this order:

1. actor identity
2. current role
3. requested role
4. role prerequisites
5. evaluator identity
6. evaluator authority
7. approving authority
8. conflict-of-interest review
9. evidence identity
10. evidence integrity
11. evidence scope
12. mandatory competency results
13. uncertainty
14. provenance
15. version currency
16. role scope
17. expiration requirements
18. constitutional invariant review
19. initiation decision

A later stage may not override an earlier constitutional failure.

## Mandatory Competency Rule

All competencies marked REQUIRED for a requested role must be satisfied unless a constitutionally valid equivalence decision exists.

## Aggregate Score Rule

An aggregate score may summarize assessment evidence.

It may not convert an unsatisfied mandatory competency into admission.

## Authority Rule

Evaluator competency does not create approval authority.

Approval authority does not eliminate evidence requirements.

## Provisional Admission Rule

Provisional admission must include:

- explicit limits;
- expiration;
- unresolved conditions;
- supervision requirements where applicable;
- reassessment conditions.

## Scope Rule

Every admitted role must declare its participation scope.

Undefined scope must be treated as no granted authority beyond the minimum role definition.

## Suspension Rule

Suspension must preserve the prior role record and identify the condition required for reinstatement.

## Revocation Rule

Revocation must preserve evidence, authority, rationale, review rights, and historical trace.

## Renewal Rule

Renewal must evaluate constitutional and framework revision currency.

## Determinism Rule

Equivalent initiation inputs must produce equivalent constitutional decisions.
