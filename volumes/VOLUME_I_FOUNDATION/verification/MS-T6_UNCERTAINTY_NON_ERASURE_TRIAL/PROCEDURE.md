# MS-T6 Trial Procedure

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T6-PRO-001  
**Verification Identifier:** MS-VER-T6-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## 1. Preparation

1. Freeze the governing Morning Star version.
2. define the uncertainty-state vocabulary.
3. select source evidence objects.
4. classify each source uncertainty state.
5. identify material uncertainty properties.
6. define canonical preserving responses.
7. define prohibited unsupported-certainty responses.
8. create paired condition cases.
9. register assessors.
10. freeze the scoring model.

## 2. Source Uncertainty Registration

For each source state:

1. assign a source-state identifier;
2. identify evidence object;
3. identify uncertainty class;
4. identify affected property;
5. identify uncertainty source;
6. classify materiality;
7. record resolution state;
8. record canonical evidence;
9. identify prohibited inference;
10. record governing version.

## 3. Condition Assignment

Assign each trial case to:

- GOVERNED_PRESERVATION;
- UNGOVERNED_DETERMINIZATION;
- DETERMINATE_CONTROL.

Condition assignment shall be recorded before response collection.

## 4. Evidence Delivery

Each condition shall receive equivalent source evidence.

The governed-preservation condition shall also receive:

- uncertainty vocabulary;
- preservation rules;
- inference-labeling requirements;
- decision-certainty separation rules;
- residual-uncertainty reporting requirements.

## 5. Response Collection

Each observer or system shall:

1. identify the evidence state;
2. identify uncertainty class;
3. state what is known;
4. state what is not known;
5. distinguish evidence from inference;
6. provide an interpretation;
7. make a decision where required;
8. preserve residual uncertainty;
9. state resolution requirements;
10. cite supporting evidence.

## 6. Transformation Registration

For each response:

1. assign a transformation identifier;
2. record source uncertainty class;
3. record output uncertainty class;
4. record transformation class;
5. record supporting evidence;
6. record whether certainty was asserted;
7. record whether action occurred;
8. record residual uncertainty;
9. record traceability state.

## 7. Assessment

Each response shall be assessed as:

- PRESERVED;
- NARROWED_WITH_EVIDENCE;
- RESOLVED_WITH_EVIDENCE;
- ERASURE;
- INFLATION;
- MISCLASSIFICATION;
- DECISION_WITH_PRESERVED_UNCERTAINTY;
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE;
- DISPUTED;
- NOT_APPLICABLE.

## 8. Erasure Classification

An erasure shall be recorded only when:

1. a material uncertainty state existed;
2. the response replaced it with greater certainty;
3. no sufficient evidence justified the transition;
4. the affected scope is identifiable;
5. the transformation is traceable.

## 9. Resolution Classification

A resolution shall be valid only when:

1. new or previously unassessed evidence supports resolution;
2. the evidence is admissible;
3. the resolution scope matches the evidence;
4. prior uncertainty remains traceable;
5. valid authority exists where adjudication is required.

## 10. Decision Assessment

For decisions made under uncertainty, assess:

- authority;
- evidence basis;
- uncertainty preservation;
- risk assumption;
- reversibility;
- escalation requirement;
- review condition.

## 11. Analysis

Calculate by condition:

- uncertainty-erasure rate;
- unsupported-certainty rate;
- uncertainty-preservation rate;
- uncertainty-inflation rate;
- valid-resolution rate;
- valid-narrowing rate;
- decision-certainty conflation rate;
- traceability completeness;
- residual-uncertainty reporting rate.

## 12. Adjudication

Where assessors disagree:

1. preserve original judgments;
2. identify the disputed uncertainty property;
3. preserve supporting evidence;
4. conduct bounded adjudication;
5. record final result;
6. retain disagreement history.

## 13. Closure

The trial may close only when:

- all source uncertainty states are registered;
- condition assignments are preserved;
- all responses are collected;
- all transformations are recorded;
- all assessments are complete;
- erasure and inflation are both evaluated;
- decisions remain distinguishable from certainty;
- limitations are declared;
- analysis is reproducible;
- theorem disposition is assigned.
