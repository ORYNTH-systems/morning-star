# MS-T6 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T6-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- uncertainty-state classification reliability;
- source-evidence quality;
- observer risk tolerance;
- decision-pressure effects;
- task wording;
- domain-specific uncertainty conventions;
- assessor subjectivity;
- resolution-evidence ambiguity;
- action-context effects;
- sample-size limitations;
- framework-specific effects;
- external-validity limits.

A result for one uncertainty class shall not automatically generalize to every domain or decision context.
