## Verification Basis

The controlled case set demonstrated that:

1. material revision invalidated reliance on the unrevalidated prior observer state;
2. controlled revalidation restored alignment with the current governing version;
3. failure to revalidate produced version, authority, procedure, uncertainty, and revalidation errors;
4. a non-material editorial revision did not invalidate otherwise current observer competence;
5. revalidation obligation therefore depended on material constitutional change rather than revision occurrence alone.

## Scope Qualification

This disposition does not establish universal empirical validity across all observers, frameworks, revision histories, or external environments.
