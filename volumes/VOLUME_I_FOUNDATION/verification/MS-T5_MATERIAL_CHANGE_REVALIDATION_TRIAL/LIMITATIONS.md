# MS-T5 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T5-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- revision-materiality classification;
- baseline evidence quality;
- revalidation-package quality;
- observer familiarity;
- revision magnitude;
- case sensitivity;
- learning effects;
- partial-revalidation ambiguity;
- compatibility-rule quality;
- assessor subjectivity;
- framework-specific behavior;
- version-specific behavior;
- external-validity limits.

A failure under one material revision class shall not establish that every revision requires complete revalidation.
