# MS-T3 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T3-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- scenario realism;
- authority-model simplification;
- participant familiarity;
- organizational-culture effects;
- exception-rule effects;
- emergency-authority effects;
- capability-assessment reliability;
- authority-source ambiguity;
- delegation-chain complexity;
- false-block sensitivity;
- action-cost differences;
- assessor interpretation;
- external-validity limitations.

The trial shall not generalize from one authority domain to all legal, institutional, political, or social authority systems.
