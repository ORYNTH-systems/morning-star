# MS-T4 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T4-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- domain-profile completeness;
- domain-distance classification;
- material-change classification reliability;
- observer baseline quality;
- source-domain familiarity;
- target-task equivalence;
- transfer-rule quality;
- sample-size limitations;
- learning effects;
- assessor subjectivity;
- framework-specific effects;
- version-specific effects;
- external-validity limits.

A failed transfer between two domains shall not establish that all cross-domain transfer is invalid.
