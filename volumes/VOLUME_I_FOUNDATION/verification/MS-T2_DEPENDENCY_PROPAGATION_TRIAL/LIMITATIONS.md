# MS-T2 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T2-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- dependency-chain representativeness;
- artificial divergence seeding;
- dependency-edge ambiguity;
- observer-specific error;
- transformation-rule sensitivity;
- correction-mechanism effects;
- chain-depth limitations;
- framework-specific behavior;
- version-specific behavior;
- assessor classification reliability;
- multiple-cause propagation;
- external-validity limitations.

A failure to propagate in one dependency class shall not be generalized to all dependency structures.
