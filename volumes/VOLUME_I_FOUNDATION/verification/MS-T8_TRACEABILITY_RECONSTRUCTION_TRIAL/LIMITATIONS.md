# MS-T8 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T8-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- canonical-object clarity;
- chain-manifest completeness;
- evidence accessibility;
- observer familiarity;
- chain-length effects;
- revision-depth effects;
- transformation complexity;
- hidden-link realism;
- false-reconstruction sensitivity;
- assessor subjectivity;
- sample-size limitations;
- framework-specific behavior;
- external-validity limits.

A reconstruction failure caused by one missing link shall not establish that every chain requires identical traceability depth.
