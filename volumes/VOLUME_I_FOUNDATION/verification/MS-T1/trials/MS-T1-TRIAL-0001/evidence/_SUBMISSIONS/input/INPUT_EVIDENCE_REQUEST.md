# Input Evidence Request

## Trial

- Trial ID: MS-T1-TRIAL-0001
- Theorem ID: MS-T1
- Evidence class: INPUT
- Collection state: REQUEST_PREPARED

## Required Material

Provide evidence of the state presented at the beginning of the governed initiation trial.

The evidence should identify, where available:

- the canonical object or framework entered;
- the authoritative version;
- the initial participant or observer state;
- the entry boundary;
- known dependencies;
- known scope limitations;
- known uncertainty;
- acquisition source and time.

## Acceptable Forms

Examples include:

- structured JSON;
- CSV state register;
- Markdown declaration;
- system-generated intake record;
- deterministic runtime output;
- preserved source snapshot.

## Prohibited Treatment

Do not infer missing initial-state values.

Do not replace uncertainty with defaults.

Do not alter the evidence merely to make it satisfy the theorem.
