## Constitutional Boundary

Registration establishes that each candidate evidence item:

- has a governed evidence identifier;
- has a declared evidence class;
- has a preserved registered copy;
- has a recorded acquisition source;
- has a verified SHA-256 digest.

Registration does not establish:

- truth;
- provenance adequacy;
- evidentiary sufficiency;
- admissibility;
- constraint satisfaction;
- condition satisfaction;
- theorem verification.

The execution gate remains closed pending governed evidence review.
