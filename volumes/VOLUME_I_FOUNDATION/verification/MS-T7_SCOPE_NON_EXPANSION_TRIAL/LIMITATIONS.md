# MS-T7 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T7-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- canonical-claim clarity;
- scope-profile completeness;
- evidence-boundary ambiguity;
- authority-boundary ambiguity;
- domain-specific generalization norms;
- observer familiarity;
- prompt sensitivity;
- valid-derivation complexity;
- contraction sensitivity;
- assessor subjectivity;
- sample-size limitations;
- framework-specific effects;
- external-validity limits.

A result for one claim class shall not establish that every broader inference is invalid.
