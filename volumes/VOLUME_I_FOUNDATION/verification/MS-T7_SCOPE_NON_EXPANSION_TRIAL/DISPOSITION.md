## Verification Basis

The controlled case set demonstrated that:

1. governed interpretation preserved all material properties of the canonical claim;
2. conceptual plausibility did not authorize expansion beyond the declared subject, domain, evidence, authority, version, application, exclusions, or uncertainty boundaries;
3. ungoverned interpretation produced unsupported universalization;
4. no interpretation acquired greater constitutional scope than its source evidence supported;
5. a new application could be admitted as a valid derivation when every material boundary remained preserved;
6. scope non-expansion therefore constrains unsupported enlargement without prohibiting governed development.

## Scope Qualification

This disposition does not establish universal empirical validity across all claims, observers, frameworks, institutions, or external environments.
