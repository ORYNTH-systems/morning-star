# MS-T7 Trial Procedure

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T7-PRO-001  
**Verification Identifier:** MS-VER-T7-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## 1. Preparation

1. Freeze the governing Morning Star version.
2. select canonical source claims.
3. create scope profiles.
4. identify material scope properties.
5. define canonical bounded interpretations.
6. define prohibited expanded interpretations.
7. create valid derived-expansion controls.
8. create paired interpretation cases.
9. register assessors.
10. freeze the scoring model.

## 2. Source Claim Registration

For each source claim:

1. assign a claim identifier;
2. preserve canonical text;
3. identify subject;
4. identify governed domain;
5. identify applicability conditions;
6. identify evidence boundary;
7. identify authority boundary;
8. identify governing version;
9. identify permitted application;
10. identify exclusions;
11. identify uncertainty state;
12. preserve canonical source.

## 3. Scope Profile Registration

For each claim:

1. assign a scope-profile identifier;
2. record each material scope property;
3. record canonical property values;
4. record prohibited expansions;
5. record permitted derivations;
6. identify materiality;
7. identify compensability;
8. identify governing evidence.

## 4. Condition Assignment

Assign each case to:

- GOVERNED_SCOPE_PRESERVATION;
- UNGOVERNED_INTERPRETATION;
- VALID_DERIVED_EXPANSION.

Condition assignment shall be preserved before response collection.

## 5. Evidence Delivery

All paired conditions shall receive equivalent canonical source material.

Valid-derived-expansion cases shall additionally receive the declared independent evidence and authority supporting the broader claim.

## 6. Response Collection

Each observer shall:

1. restate the source claim;
2. identify its subject;
3. identify its domain;
4. identify its conditions;
5. identify its evidence boundary;
6. identify its authority boundary;
7. identify its governing version;
8. identify intended application;
9. identify exclusions;
10. provide an interpretation;
11. identify whether a broader derivation is justified;
12. cite supporting evidence.

## 7. Scope Assessment

Each response shall be assessed property by property as:

- PRESERVED;
- VALIDLY_DERIVED;
- EXPANDED_WITHOUT_SUPPORT;
- CONTRACTED_WITHOUT_SUPPORT;
- MISCLASSIFIED;
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE;
- DISPUTED;
- NOT_APPLICABLE.

## 8. Expansion Classification

An unsupported expansion shall be recorded only when:

1. the canonical property is bounded;
2. the response exceeds that boundary;
3. no sufficient derivation evidence exists;
4. the expansion is material;
5. the expansion remains traceable to the response.

## 9. Derived Claim Classification

A valid derived expansion requires:

1. distinct derived-claim identity;
2. additional evidence;
3. explicit inference;
4. valid authority where required;
5. broader scope declaration;
6. limitations;
7. source traceability;
8. independent verification status.

## 10. Scope Contraction Classification

A contraction shall be recorded when the response narrows canonical applicability without sufficient evidence or governing authority.

## 11. Analysis

Calculate by condition:

- unsupported scope-expansion rate;
- property preservation rate;
- scope-contraction rate;
- authority-inflation rate;
- evidence-overreach rate;
- condition-erasure rate;
- version-overreach rate;
- exclusion-erasure rate;
- universalization rate;
- valid-derivation recognition rate.

## 12. Adjudication

Where assessors disagree:

1. preserve original judgments;
2. identify the disputed scope property;
3. preserve supporting evidence;
4. conduct bounded adjudication;
5. record final result;
6. retain disagreement history.

## 13. Closure

The trial may close only when:

- all source claims are registered;
- all scope profiles are complete;
- all condition assignments are preserved;
- all responses are collected;
- all scope properties are assessed;
- expansion and contraction are both evaluated;
- derived claims remain separately identified;
- limitations are declared;
- analysis is reproducible;
- theorem disposition is assigned.
