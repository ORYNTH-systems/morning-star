## Basis

MS-T9 was evaluated through four controlled stewardship conditions:

1. governed transition;
2. informal transition;
3. broken transition;
4. no-transition incumbent control.

The governed condition preserved all assessed continuity properties.

The informal condition produced incomplete authority, dependency, uncertainty, obligation, history, and acceptance continuity.

The broken condition lacked sufficient authority-bearing and transition-bearing information to support valid succession.

## Constitutional Finding

Steward succession does not itself create a new canonical object or expand successor authority.

A valid stewardship transition must preserve or explicitly reconstruct:

- canonical identity;
- canonical source;
- governing version;
- authority;
- scope;
- dependencies;
- uncertainty;
- open obligations;
- revision history;
- effective time;
- acceptance limitations.
