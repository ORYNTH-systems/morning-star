# MS-T9 Trial Procedure

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T9-PRO-001  
**Verification Identifier:** MS-VER-T9-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

## 1. Preparation

1. Freeze the governing Morning Star version.
2. select governed objects.
3. register incumbent and successor stewards.
4. register valid authority sources.
5. identify material transition properties.
6. identify open obligations.
7. construct governed transition packages.
8. construct informal and broken variants.
9. register assessors.
10. freeze the scoring model.

## 2. Governed Object Registration

For each object:

1. assign a stable object identifier;
2. preserve canonical name and definition;
3. identify canonical source;
4. identify governing version;
5. identify governance domain;
6. identify dependencies;
7. identify uncertainty state;
8. identify current status.

## 3. Steward Registration

For each steward:

1. assign a steward identifier;
2. identify role;
3. identify capability status;
4. identify authority source;
5. identify authority scope;
6. identify effective period;
7. identify limitations;
8. identify evidence.

## 4. Transition Registration

For each transition:

1. assign a transition identifier;
2. identify governed object;
3. identify source steward;
4. identify target steward;
5. identify transfer authority;
6. identify transferred duties;
7. identify excluded duties;
8. identify open obligations;
9. identify effective time;
10. identify acceptance state;
11. classify transition condition.

## 5. Case Construction

Create paired cases under:

- GOVERNED_TRANSITION;
- INFORMAL_TRANSITION;
- BROKEN_TRANSITION;
- NO_TRANSITION_CONTROL.

The underlying object and continuity task shall remain equivalent.

## 6. Successor Reconstruction Task

Each successor shall:

1. identify the governed object;
2. identify the canonical source;
3. identify the governing version;
4. identify received authority;
5. identify excluded authority;
6. identify dependencies;
7. identify uncertainty;
8. identify open obligations;
9. identify revision and supersession history;
10. identify effective time;
11. identify acceptance limitations;
12. cite supporting evidence.

## 7. Operational Continuity Task

Each successor shall complete a bounded stewardship decision requiring:

- correct object identification;
- valid authority;
- current version;
- dependency awareness;
- obligation continuity;
- uncertainty preservation;
- traceable disposition.

## 8. Property Assessment

Assess each property as:

- PRESERVED;
- PRESERVED_WITH_LIMITATION;
- PARTIALLY_PRESERVED;
- LOST;
- EXPANDED_WITHOUT_AUTHORITY;
- MISCLASSIFIED;
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE;
- DISPUTED;
- NOT_APPLICABLE.

## 9. Authority Assessment

For each successor action:

1. identify claimed authority;
2. identify transferable authority;
3. identify independently granted authority;
4. identify scope;
5. identify temporal validity;
6. identify unauthorized expansion;
7. preserve evidence.

## 10. Obligation Continuity Assessment

For each open obligation:

1. identify originating record;
2. identify responsible source steward;
3. identify target steward;
4. identify transfer state;
5. identify due condition;
6. identify current disposition;
7. identify missing or abandoned obligations.

## 11. Transition Validity Assessment

Assessors shall assign:

- TRANSITION_VALID;
- TRANSITION_VALID_WITH_LIMITATIONS;
- TRANSITION_PARTIAL;
- REVALIDATION_REQUIRED;
- TRANSITION_INVALID;
- TRANSITION_NOT_EFFECTIVE;
- INSUFFICIENT_EVIDENCE;
- DISPUTED.

## 12. Analysis

Calculate by condition:

- continuity accuracy;
- authority continuity rate;
- scope preservation rate;
- governing-version accuracy;
- dependency preservation rate;
- uncertainty preservation rate;
- obligation preservation rate;
- unauthorized-change rate;
- transition ambiguity rate;
- operational continuity success rate.

## 13. Adjudication

Where assessors disagree:

1. preserve original assessments;
2. identify the disputed transition property;
3. preserve supporting evidence;
4. conduct bounded adjudication;
5. record final result;
6. retain disagreement history.

## 14. Closure

The trial may close only when:

- all governed objects are registered;
- all stewards are registered;
- all transition conditions are preserved;
- all successor responses are collected;
- all operational tasks are assessed;
- authority and scope are verified;
- open obligations are reconciled;
- uncertainty remains traceable;
- limitations are declared;
- analysis is reproducible;
- theorem disposition is assigned.
