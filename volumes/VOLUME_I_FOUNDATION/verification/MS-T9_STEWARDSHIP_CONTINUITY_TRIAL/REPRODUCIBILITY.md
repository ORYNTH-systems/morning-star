# MS-T9 Reproducibility Requirements

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T9-REP-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

A reproduction package shall include:

1. governing Morning Star commit;
2. theorem version;
3. trial design;
4. procedure;
5. data dictionary;
6. scoring model;
7. governed objects;
8. canonical sources;
9. steward profiles;
10. authority evidence;
11. stewardship transfers;
12. transferred and excluded duties;
13. open-obligation records;
14. acceptance records;
15. continuity cases;
16. successor responses;
17. operational decisions;
18. continuity assessments;
19. adjudication records;
20. analysis procedure;
21. protocol deviations;
22. limitations;
23. final disposition.

A result is reproducible only when an independent evaluator can reconstruct every transition and assessment from preserved object, authority, obligation, acceptance, response, and evidence records.
