# MS-T9 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T9-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- governed-object complexity;
- source-authority clarity;
- successor capability variation;
- transition-package quality;
- obligation-register completeness;
- operational-task equivalence;
- emergency-transition effects;
- acceptance ambiguity;
- revision-depth effects;
- assessor subjectivity;
- sample-size limitations;
- framework-specific behavior;
- external-validity limits.

A failure in one stewardship transition shall not establish that all succession requires an identical transition mechanism.
