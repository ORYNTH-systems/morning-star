# MS-T1 Limitations

**Document Identifier:** MS-V1-T1-LIM-001  
**Version:** 0.1.0

The trial shall evaluate and report:

- observer-selection effects;
- prior-domain familiarity;
- case-set representativeness;
- assessor subjectivity;
- learning effects;
- condition contamination;
- sample-size limitations;
- framework-specific effects;
- version-specific effects;
- artificial trial conditions;
- scoring reliability;
- external-validity limits.

Limitations shall not be used to erase admissible results, but they shall constrain the scope of any theorem disposition.
